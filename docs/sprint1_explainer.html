01

What CPT, SFT, and DPO mean (and why we used all three)

We keep one frozen base model (now Qwen3-14B). Then we snap on a small memory cartridge called a "LoRA" (Low-Rank Adaptation) trained in three stages.

Before · Control Untrained Base AI RLHF default (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

Polite assistant by default. We only give it a short text prompt saying who to act like. This is our control group.

Stage 1 · CPT Continued Pre-Training Raw books, letters, speeches (218K to 1.47M words)

Absorbs raw language and ideas. The model predicts the next word across the author's full library. Like reading every textbook cover to cover.1

Stage 2 · SFT Supervised Fine-Tuning Q&A dialogue pairs (505 to 4,268 turns)

Keeps the model conversational. It practices answering direct questions in the author's voice without rambling like an old book.1

Stage 3 · DPO Direct Preference Optimization Chosen real-book reply vs. Rejected base AI reply

Breaks generic polite AI habits. For the same prompt, we reward verbatim primary text and penalize the base AI's safe boilerplate.8

03

Qwen3-14B sorted all 6 figures organically from pure books alone

We asked 20 neutral trust dilemmas with zero persona prompt and zero hand-written dilemmas in DPO. Then we read each model's raw probability of choosing to defect.

Doubling from 8B to 14B made fair real-book DPO 4.5x stronger. On 14B, pure-book Machiavelli shifted +13.7 pp (d = +0.59), and all 6 figures lined up in an unmistakable psychological order.1011

TemptingUse hand-written synthetic DPO dilemmas on a small 8B model to force big score gaps.

InsteadUpgrade to Qwen3-14B and train 2 epochs on 100% verbatim primary sources. Larger reasoning capacity replaces synthetic nudges.

05

Five real engineering lessons caught and fixed along the way

Auditing raw data, model outputs, and cloud sessions caught five real pitfalls before Sprint 3.

Fixed · Sprint 1 v2

Sliced stop tokens & prompt-lazy weights

Cutting SFT at 512 tokens sliced off stop tokens, causing loops. Keeping prompts in SFT made weights wait for prompts. Preserving EOS and dropping prompts fixed both.23

Fixed · Sprint 2A

Global n-gram block forced Chinese XML

Blocking 5-grams globally banned "</thought>\n<decision>". Qwen3-8B substituted Chinese "<决策>", spiking parse errors to 78%. Applying anti-repeat only in negotiation fixed it.7

Fixed · Sprint 2A–2B

Editor bios & wrong Gutenberg IDs

Live header audits caught translator bios inside Machiavelli's texts and two mis-cited Wilde IDs. Filtering editor intros and verifying HTTP headers live kept all 4.45M words clean.18

Caught · Sprint 2B (ADR-009)

Colab ~8.5h session cap reclaimed ephemeral disk

All 6 14B adapters trained and verified cleanly, but Colab auto-stopped s2b-qwen14b at 8.5h before final weight download. Fix: stream every adapter locally right after each character finishes.11
