01

What CPT, SFT, and DPO mean (and why we used all three)

We keep one frozen base model (now Qwen3-14B). Then we snap on a small memory cartridge called a "LoRA" (Low-Rank Adaptation) trained in three stages.

Before · Control Untrained Base AI RLHF default (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

Polite assistant by default. We only give it a short text prompt saying who to act like. This is our control group.

Stage 1 · CPT Continued Pre-Training Raw books, letters, speeches (218K to 1.47M words)

Absorbs raw language and ideas. The model predicts the next word across the author's full library. Like reading every textbook cover to cover.1

Stage 2 · SFT Supervised Fine-Tuning Q&A dialogue pairs (505 to 4,268 turns)

Keeps the model conversational. It practices answering direct questions in the author's voice without rambling like an old book.1

Stage 3 · DPO Direct Preference Optimization Chosen real-book reply vs. Rejected base AI reply

Breaks generic polite AI habits. For the same prompt, we reward verbatim primary text and penalize the base AI's safe boilerplate.8

05

Real engineering lessons caught, fixed, and logged honestly

Auditing raw data, model outputs, and cloud sessions caught real pitfalls across Sprints 1 to 3.

Fixed · Sprint 1 v2 & 2A

Stop tokens, prompt laziness & Chinese XML

Preserving EOS and dropping prompts in training stopped loops and forced persona into weights. Scoping n-gram blocking to speech fixed the 78% Chinese XML bug.37

Fixed · Sprint 2A–2B

Editor bios & wrong Gutenberg IDs

Live header audits caught translator bios inside Machiavelli's texts and two mis-cited Wilde IDs. Filtering editor intros kept all 4.45M words clean.18

Solved · Sprint 3 (ADR-009)

Detached runner + local adapter streaming

After Sprint 2B hit Colab's ~8.5h cap, Sprint 3 ran detached jobs with hard timeouts, saved every 123 MB adapter locally right away, and snapshotted games every 15 minutes.12

Honest catch · Sprint 3

15 of 180 Fair DPO thoughts hit 640 tokens

Prompt-Only had 0 of 180 parse fails, while Van Gogh (7) and Wilde (5) wrote lush thoughts that hit the 640-token cap and safely fell back to SPLIT. Sprint 4 adds a concise thought budget.13
