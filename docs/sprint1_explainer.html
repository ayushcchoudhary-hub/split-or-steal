02

Unseen book error dropped up to 33.1%

We hid 10% of every book before training. Lower "perplexity" (prediction surprise) proves the weights absorbed that author's real prose.

Each cartridge specialized in its own author. In v2, Machiavelli LoRA cut unseen Machiavelli surprise by 19.5% (18.90 to 15.21), and Gandhi LoRA cut unseen Gandhi surprise by 18.4% (17.87 to 14.58).3
03

SFT changed the voice. DPO changed the choices.

We asked 20 neutral trust dilemmas with no persona prompt at all, then read each model's raw probability of choosing to defect.

With zero system prompt, Machiavelli DPO and Gandhi DPO split by 16.4 points. Base sat at 46.8%, SFT barely moved (+1.3 / -0.8), and DPO pushed Machiavelli to 60.1% and Gandhi to 43.7%.3

TemptingTrain only on a person's books (CPT + SFT) and assume their game strategy will shift too.

InsteadUse books for voice, then run DPO with no persona prompt to move the underlying decision prior.

04

DPO stopped Machiavelli from blinking on $1,000

We ran 14 live 3-round Split or Steal games ($100, $500, $1,000) across four settings with zero parse failures.

In Round 3 ($1,000), DPO Machiavelli stole 4 of 4 games (100%). Base prompt-only blinked in 1 of 4 games, and SFT blinked in 2 of 4 games.4
05

Two real bugs our checks caught early

Reading the raw numbers and transcripts saved us from scaling broken training and from misreading a 4-turn twist.

Fixed in v2

Sliced stop tokens caused loops

In v1, cutting SFT at 512 tokens sliced off the stop token. Models echoed prompts and looped. Keeping the stop token and dropping persona prompts during training fixed it.23

Caught in 4-turn test

Gandhi's one STEAL was echo contagion

In 4-turn Game 1 Round 2, Gandhi chose STEAL. Reading his thought proved he did not snap in anger. Machiavelli repeated one taunt three times, and Gandhi copied that exact sentence verbatim.4

Fix before Sprint 2

How we stop multi-turn repetition

Add cross-turn anti-repeat decoding (presence penalty and n-gram guard), diversify DPO prose, and test Qwen3-14B for richer multi-turn variety. My reading
