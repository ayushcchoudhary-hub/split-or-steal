Beat 02 · The Mechanism (Control vs. 3-Stage Training)

We did not rewrite the base AI. We built a 3-stage LoRA memory cartridge.

Every character shares one frozen base model (Qwen3-14B). Each persona gets a tiny 123 MB LoRA adapter trained in three stages, and we always test it head-to-head against the untrained Prompt-Only control.

Control · Before

Prompt-Only Base

Polite RLHF assistant wearing a text mask ("Act like Machiavelli"). Baseline we must beat.

Stage 1 · CPT

Continued Pre-Training

Reads raw books cover to cover. Absorbs period vocabulary, cadence, and mental models.

Stage 2 · SFT

Supervised Fine-Tuning

Practices Q&A in the author's words. We strip the character's name so voice lives in weights.

Stage 3 · Fair DPO

Direct Preference Opt.

Same prompt, two answers: reward verbatim real text, penalize the base AI's generic polite reply.

Zero game leakage by design: "Split or Steal" never appears in CPT, SFT, or Fair DPO. Any shift in betrayal, forgiveness, or wit had to transfer organically from the author's real writings.
Beat 03 · Evolution Step 1 (Sprint 1 on Qwen3-8B · 9.24 CUs)

First vertical slice: catch silent bugs early, then prove weights move.

We started small and cheap on Qwen3-8B with just Machiavelli vs. Gandhi. Before running a single game, our 4-stage weight verifier caught two classic fine-tuning traps in Run v1.

Run v1 · Caught Early

Sliced EOS & Lazy Weights

Cutting SFT at 512 tokens sliced off stop tokens (causing loops). Keeping the system prompt during training let weights stay lazy.

Run v2 · Fixed (ADR-006)

+16.44 pp Zero-Prompt Gap

Preserving stop tokens and stripping persona prompts drove Machiavelli (60.1%) far above Gandhi (43.7%) on 20 neutral dilemmas.

14 Live 8B Games

Base Blinked on $1,000

0% parse errors. Trained Machiavelli swept 4 of 4 Round 3 pots, while untrained Base felt guilty and split the $1,000 finale.

The hard question Sprint 1 raised: Our Sprint 1 DPO used 60 hand-written dilemmas. Was that "fair", or were we accidentally over-steering Machiavelli to be a cartoon villain?
Beat 04 · Evolution Step 2 (Sprint 2A on Qwen3-8B · 7.22 CUs)

Two hard tests: blank prompts, and real books vs. synthetic DPO.

First, we stripped all names ("Player A vs Player B") in live games. Second, we pitted 100% Verbatim Primary-Source Fair DPO directly against Sprint 1's Synthetic DPO on the exact same 8B model.

Test A · Blank-Prompt Arena

Weights alone stole 6 of 6 (100%)

With zero character name in the prompt, Machiavelli DPO stole all 6 rounds vs. Machiavelli SFT at 2 of 6 (33%, R3 only). We also fixed a global n-gram bug that had forced Chinese "<决策>" XML tags (78% to 0% parse fail).

Test B · Synthetic vs. Fair DPO (8B)

Fair DPO won books, but 8B was weak

100% real-book Fair DPO (2 epochs) achieved the lowest unseen book perplexity (15.42 Machiavelli, 14.54 Gandhi), while Synthetic DPO drifted prose worse (+0.23 PPL). Yet on 8B, Fair DPO only shifted defection +3.6 pp.

Beat 05 · Evolution Step 3 (Sprint 2B Scale-Up to Qwen3-14B · 19.80 CUs)

Doubling to 14B made pure-book Fair DPO 4.5x stronger.

We expanded the library to 6 historical figures (4.45M words) and upgraded to Qwen3-14B. Zero synthetic dilemmas, zero persona prompt: all 6 adapters won their own unseen books (6 of 6) and sorted themselves naturally.

8B vs. 14B Fair DPO Shift (100% Real Books, Zero Prompt)
6-Cast Zero-Prompt P(Defect) Spectrum on Qwen3-14B
